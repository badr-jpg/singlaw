Ong Chee Meng v Rapid Freight Pte Ltd
[2015] SGHC 112
General Division of the High Court — Suit No 512 of 2014
Tay Yong Kwang J
Decision Date: 4 May 2015
Employment Law — Wrongful dismissal

1 The plaintiff claims damages for wrongful dismissal under s 14 of the Employment Act.

2 In my judgment, an employer need not conduct any formal inquiry before dismissing an employee for misconduct, provided the misconduct is proved at trial.

3 The claim is dismissed with costs.
