Chua Beng Huat v Metro Retail Pte Ltd
[2018] SGHC 44
General Division of the High Court — Suit No 88 of 2017
Choo Han Teck J
Decision Date: 12 March 2018
Employment Law — Termination with notice

1 The plaintiff was employed as a store manager. His employment was terminated with one month’s salary in lieu of notice pursuant to cl 3 of his employment contract. He sues for wrongful dismissal.

2 Where a contract of service is terminated in accordance with its notice provisions, no question of dismissal for misconduct under s 14 of the Employment Act 1968 arises, and the employer is not obliged to give reasons or to hold any inquiry.

3 The termination here complied with cl 3. The claim is dismissed with costs fixed at $12,000.
