Employment (Part-Time Employees) Regulations

Current version as at 01 Apr 2026

Date of commencement: 1 October 1996

Citation

1. These Regulations are the Employment (Part-Time Employees) Regulations.

Definitions

2. In these Regulations, "part-time employee" means an employee who is required under his contract of service with an employer to work for less than 35 hours a week.

Annual leave

4.—(1) A part-time employee who has served an employer for a period of not less than 3 months is entitled to paid annual leave in proportion to that of a similar full-time employee, based on the number of hours the part-time employee is required to work.

(2) The entitlement under paragraph (1) is to be calculated in accordance with the formula set out in the Schedule.

Rest days and public holidays

5. A part-time employee is entitled to pay in lieu of public holidays calculated in proportion to the hours the employee is required to work compared with a similar full-time employee.
