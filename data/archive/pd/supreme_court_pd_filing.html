Supreme Court Practice Directions 2021 — Part 6: Originating Processes

39. Unless otherwise provided in these Practice Directions or directed by the Court, every originating application must be filed through the Electronic Filing Service.

40. An originating claim must be served on the defendant within the period of its validity, and the claimant must file proof of service within 14 days after service is effected.

41. A party who is unable to comply with any timeline in these Practice Directions must, before the timeline expires, apply for an extension of time, stating the reasons for the inability to comply.

42. Where a defendant intends to contest an originating claim, the defendant must file and serve a notice of intention to contest or not contest within 14 days after the statement of claim is served (or 21 days if served out of Singapore).
