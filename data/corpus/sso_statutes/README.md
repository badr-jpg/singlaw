Singapore legislation (Acts; subsidiary legislation available but not indexed by default) from the
Hugging Face dataset https://huggingface.co/datasets/Kaidon-Ng/singapore-statutes
("LawBot Project", 2026), parsed by its authors from Singapore Statutes Online PDFs.

Reused under clause 13 of the SSO Terms of Use (https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Terms-of-Use):
- Singapore legislation is subject to copyright of the Singapore Government.
- Check Singapore Statutes Online (https://sso.agc.gov.sg) for the latest version.
This text is not authoritative; the Government Gazette is (s 48 Interpretation Act 1965
does not apply to material copied from SSO). The copy may contain PDF-parsing errors and
may not reflect recent amendments; repealed legislation is not included.

Files: sections.parquet / documents.parquet (as downloaded) and their .jsonl conversions
(scripts/prepare_sso_statutes.py). Chunking: backend/app/ingest/sso_statutes.py.
